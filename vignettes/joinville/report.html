Fitted Models

  • Initial period: 2020-W09 to 2025-W01
  • In all models, for each response variable \(Y_t\), we assume a Poisson distribution with mean \(\lambda_t\) and log link function, and we include a random walk of order 1 in the time observations, with cyclic effect for the epidemiological week and an AR(1) structure for the year, as follows:

\[\begin{align*} Y_t &\sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda_t), \\ \log(\lambda_t) &= \beta_0 + \beta_1 X_{t} + u_t + v_t(\text{week}_t, \text{year}_t) \\ u_t &\sim \text{RW1}(\sigma^2_u), \\ \end{align*}\]

where for a fixed year \(y\), \[\begin{align*} v_t(\text{week}_t, y) &\sim \text{RW1}(\sigma^2_w), \\ v_t(1, y) - v_t(52, y) &\sim \text{Normal}(0, \sigma^2_w), \end{align*}\] and for a fixed epidemiological week \(w\), \[\begin{align*} v_t(w, \text{year}_t) &\sim \text{AR1}(\rho, \sigma^2_y). \end{align*}\]
