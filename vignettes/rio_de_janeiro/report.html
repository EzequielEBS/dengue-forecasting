Fitting Models

Initial period: 2023-W13 to 2025-W18

Model 1

For each observed case \(Y_i\), we assume a Poisson distribution with mean \(\mu_i\): \[Y_i \sim \text{Poi}(\mu_i).\] The mean \(\mu_i\) is modeled as a function of the covariates using a log link function: \[ \begin{align*} \log(\mu_i) &= \beta_0 + \beta_1 \cdot \text{tempmed}_i + \beta_2 \cdot \text{umidmed}_i + u_i, \\ u_i &\sim \text{RW1}(\sigma^2_u). \end{align*} \]

Model 2

We change the model 1 by including lagged effects of the covariates: \[ \begin{align*} \log(\mu_i) &= \beta_0 + \beta_1 \cdot \text{tempmed}_{i-5} + \beta_3 \cdot \text{umidmed}_{i-8} + u_i, \\ u_i &\sim \text{RW1}(\sigma^2_u). \end{align*} \]
